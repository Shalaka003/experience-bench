Outfits



Day 1 Nightwear: A night suit paired with a Purdue t-shirt.

Beach Outfit: A standard t-shirt and shorts.

Flight Outfit: A warm outfit intended to be worn on the plane rather than packed.

Friend's Outfit: A beige outfit you are packing specifically to give to a friend on the trip.

Spare Clothing: An extra t-shirt packed in case you get wet at the beach.

Things to Remember

Wear your designated flight outfit to the airport so you aren't cold.

Buy foundation in a neutral shade before the trip.

Message your friend to check their flight times and coordinate when they are landing.
